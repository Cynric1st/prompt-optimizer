Privacy Policy · 生效日期 Effective 2026-10-02 · 扶摇提示词优化 FUYAO PROMPT（prompt.iforworlds.com）

1. 本地处理 Local Processing

本工具的全部处理均在您的浏览器本地完成。您输入的文本与生成的提示词不会上传到任何服务器，我们看不到、也无法访问您的内容。

2. 数据收集 Data Collection

本站不需要注册，不收集任何个人身份信息。部分设置（如界面语言）仅保存在您浏览器的 localStorage 中，可随时清除。

3. 广告 Advertising

本站广告由第三方广告联盟 Adsterra 投放（通过 Service Worker 加载）。Adsterra 及其广告主会使用 Cookie，根据您访问本站及其他网站的记录投放广告。您可以清除浏览器数据（含 Service Worker 注册）以停止个性化广告。

Privacy Policy (English)

All processing in this tool happens entirely in your browser. The text you enter and the prompts you generate are never uploaded to any server; we cannot see or access your content.

The site requires no registration and collects no personally identifiable information. Some preferences (such as interface language) are stored only in your browser's localStorage and can be cleared at any time.

Ads on this site are served by the third-party ad network Adsterra (loaded via a service worker). Adsterra and its advertisers use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can clear your browser data (including the service worker registration) to stop personalized ads.
